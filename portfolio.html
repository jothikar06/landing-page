Case study

From 10K to 105K, and from followers to sales

How content strategy and automation grew a spiritual brand and kept its product sales and yoga class bookings coming in.

Aathi: Spiritual Products & Yoga

InstagramYouTube2023 – 2025
10K→105K followers in 2 years, plus more product sales and yoga programme bookings

The challenge

The brand sells spiritual products and runs 1-month and 3-month yoga programmes. It needed a bigger audience, and that audience had to turn into buyers and students, not just likes.

The strategy

  • A consistent posting schedule, timed to when the audience is most active
  • Daily stories to keep followers engaged
  • ManyChat comment automation: comment a keyword, get the product or class link in DMs
  • Automated replies to frequently asked questions so no lead waited
  • Content that tied spiritual value to the products and classes

The results

  • 10× follower growth: 10K → 105K
  • More sales of spiritual products from social
  • Steady bookings for 1-month & 3-month yoga programmes
